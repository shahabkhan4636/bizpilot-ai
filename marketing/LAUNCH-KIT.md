## 1. One-line positioning

> **BizPilot AI turns a stack of enquiries into signed proposals — without a spreadsheet.**
> Leads, follow-ups, proposals and the client's signature in one workspace.

**Who it is for (pick one and aim only at it for 14 days):** interior designers & contractors, small
marketing agencies, property/real-estate agents, IT/software consultancies, event planners — businesses
that chase 20–200 enquiries a month and lose deals to slow follow-up.

**The pain, in their words:** "I forget to follow up." · "My proposals are in Word and I can't tell who
opened them." · "Everything is in my WhatsApp and my head."

## 2. Landing page copy (headline → CTA)

**H1:** Every lead followed up. Every proposal signed. No spreadsheet.
**Sub:** BizPilot AI is the CRM for small teams: it captures enquiries, tells you who to call today,
sends the proposal, and records the client's acceptance — with an AI assistant that drafts the message
while you are still on the phone.
**CTA:** Start free for 14 days — no card needed.
**Trust strip:** Cancel anytime · Your data stays in your workspace · Works on the phone in your pocket
**Three blocks:**
1. **Nothing slips** — an overdue follow-up list that cannot be ignored, because it is generated from the
   database, not from your memory. *(Professional and above can auto-create the next task.)*
2. **Proposals that close** — a link your client opens; accept or decline with a reason, timestamped,
   with who signed. Printed to a real PDF if they want paper.
3. **A team, not a shared login** — invite your staff with roles: owner, admin, manager, member, viewer.
   A viewer cannot change anything, and the server refuses even if they try.

**Pricing table:** put the four plans side by side, with one line each:
Starter $29 — 1 user, 100 leads, proposals, documents.
Professional $79 — 3 users, 1,000 leads, **automations**, AI drafts.
Business $149 — 10 users, 5,000 leads, advanced analytics, 50 automations.
Agency $299 — 50 users, 25,000 leads, multi-workspace, white label.
*Every plan: 14-day trial, cancel in one click.*

## 3. Cold outreach — 4 scripts

### A. Email (day 1) — send 10/day, personalised line first
> Subject: `{{Company}} — who follows up on your enquiries?`
>
> Hi {{FirstName}},
>
> I noticed {{specific detail: e.g. "you take enquiries through Instagram DMs"}}.
>
> Most {{niche}} businesses I speak to lose 2–3 deals a month simply because nobody chased them after
> day 3. I built a small tool that fixes exactly that: every enquiry gets an owner, a follow-up date and a
> reminder, and the proposal goes out as one link the client can accept in a click.
>
> Want me to set it up with your own 5 current enquiries, free, so you can see the follow-up list working?
> Takes 10 minutes on a call.
>
> {{YourName}} · {{phone}} · {{yourdomain}}

### B. WhatsApp / DM (short, because people read these on the phone)
> Hi {{Name}}, {{YourName}} here. Quick question — when a new enquiry comes in for {{Company}}, who
> chases it if they don't reply by day 3? I've got a tool that does that automatically and shows a daily
> "call these people" list. Free 14 days if you want to try it on real leads. Should I send the link?

### C. Follow-up (day 3, if no reply) — one line, no guilt
> Hi {{FirstName}}, did this land at a bad time? Happy to send a 60-second screen recording instead of a
> call if that's easier.

### D. Post-demo close (same day as the call)
> Thanks {{FirstName}} — I've put your 5 enquiries in. Today's follow-up list is already showing
> {{N}} people to call. The trial runs to {{date}}; if it's saving you time by then, Professional is
> $79/month and you can cancel from the billing page in one click. Anything that feels clunky, tell me
> and I'll change it this week.

## 4. Demo script (10 minutes, keep to the clock)

| Min | What you do | What you say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–1 | Ask | "Aap ke paas mahine mein kitni enquiry aati hain, aur abhi kahan likhi hoti hain?" |
| 1–3 | Add their real enquiry live | "Ye 20 second. Ab is ka owner hai, aaj ki date hai." Show the follow-up date. |
| 3–5 | Go to the dashboard | "Ye list aap ke liye bani hai — jo aaj call karne hain. Ye memory se nahi, database se aati hai." |
| 5–7 | Create a proposal in front of them | "Do line items, PDF ya link. Client link kholta hai aur accept karta hai — aap ko pata chalta hai **kis ne** accept kiya aur kab." |
| 7–8 | Automation (Professional) | "Proposal accept ho to automatically task ban jaye: kickoff call. Aap ko kuch karna nahi." |
| 8–9 | Invite/roles | "Aap ka staff apna login use karega; viewer sirf dekh sakta hai." |
| 9–10 | Ask for the trial | "Aap ke 5 asli leads main abhi daal deta hoon, 14 din free, card nahi. Theek hai?" |

**Never** demo the AI or email features with no API key configured — the app will honestly say "not
configured" and that is a bad first impression. Either configure the keys or skip those screens.

## 5. Objection handling

| They say | You say |
| --- | --- |
| "We already use Excel/WhatsApp." | "Great — keep it. This just makes sure nothing rots in it: every enquiry gets an owner and a date, and you get one list each morning." |
| "Too expensive." | "One extra closed deal pays for the year. Starter is $29 and if it doesn't save you an hour a month, cancel from the billing page." |
| "Is my data safe?" | "Your workspace is isolated at the database level — every query is scoped to your organisation, and Postgres row-level security is on as a second lock. You can export your leads to CSV any time." |
| "Can my staff break it?" | "Invite them as a viewer or member. The permission is enforced on the server, not just hidden in the UI." |
| "AI is a gimmick." | "Then ignore it — the follow-up list and the proposal link are the product. AI just drafts the first message if you switch it on." |
| "I'll think about it." | "Fair. Let me load your 5 real enquiries now so thinking is based on your data, not my slides. If it's not useful in 14 days, it costs you nothing." |

## 7. 7-day content plan (organic, free)

1. "Why your enquiries die on day 3" — the follow-up rule (post + screenshot of the overdue list).
2. Before/after: a proposal in Word vs a link that shows who accepted.
3. Screen recording (60 s): enquiry → follow-up → proposal → accepted.
4. "The 5 numbers every small agency should know" (pipeline value, won value, overdue follow-ups…)
5. Customer story as soon as you have one, with their permission, in their words.
6. "How we handle your data" — plain-language trust post.
7. Offer post: 14-day free setup with your own leads, 5 slots.

Post where your niche already is (LinkedIn for agencies, Instagram for interior/events, local Facebook
groups for trades) — not all five everywhere.
